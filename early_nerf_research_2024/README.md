## Research Summary
We compared two NeRF data input modes: sparse single images and continuous video frames for 3D avatar reconstruction.
Experiments verified that vanilla NeRF has obvious performance defects: massive ray sampling calculation and slow rendering speed.
This bottleneck becomes the core motivation for my later CUDA & parallel computing learning and optimization development.
